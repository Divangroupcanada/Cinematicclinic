Two ways to work, both fast:

Straight off the picture — hover any thumbnail and eight numbered chips appear over it. One click files the film. No playback, no new tab.

Review mode — click the play button. The film plays right here at full size, and the moment you tag it the next one loads by itself. 1–8 to tag, → to skip.

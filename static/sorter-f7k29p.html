Two questions per film

What it is — tags, and a film can carry several. A brand film shot cinematically is Cinematic + Branding. A treatment explained on camera is Informative + Procedure. A plain procedure shot with no styling is just Procedure.

Where it goes — one choice only:

Hero — the one film behind the homepage headline. Pick one.
Home — shown on the home page. Six horizontal and about fourteen vertical is the right number.
Page — shown on the service page its tags point to.
Archive — stays on the Films page only. This is the default and it's fine for most.
Hidden — kept off the site entirely.

1–8 toggle a tag  ·  H hero   M home   P page   A archive   X hide
J K move  ·  Space play it big  ·  0 clear this film

Setting a placement in review mode moves you to the next film — so tag first, then place, and it flows.
